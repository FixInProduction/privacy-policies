Privacy Policy

WP Field, by Wear Parts & Equipment Co.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Overview

WP Field is a private enterprise application developed by Wear Parts & Equipment Co. for internal use by its sales team. It is distributed through the Apple App Store as an unlisted app. It is available only by direct link to authorized Wear Parts & Equipment Co. personnel, and it does not appear in public App Store search, categories, or charts.

Data We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with your Apple ID, we receive your email address, which identifies your account. Your name as shown in the app may be visible to your colleagues, for example next to contacts you add.

Location Data: For in-person call reports, WP Field records your location so the report reflects where the visit took place. That location is saved with the report and synced to our servers as map coordinates and, when it can be resolved, a street address (looked up through Apple's location services). The app also notes basic details about the reading: how accurate and how recent it was, and whether location access was on. If no location is available, the report still saves and is marked unverified. Your managers can see the location saved with a report, and may open it in Apple Maps. Location is used the same way when you open Nearby Customers or use CarPlay to surface nearby accounts and prospects. WP Field never reads your location in the background or continuously. It reads your location only when you create an in-person report, open Nearby Customers, or view nearby accounts in CarPlay.

Call Report Content: Notes, customer selections, the call purpose, call outcomes, and follow-up items you enter are stored as part of your call reports.

Contacts: Contact details you enter, scan from a business card, or import from your iPhone Contacts are saved with the customer account and synced to our servers as part of Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s customer records. These details include names, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, and websites. When you add a contact to your iPhone, WP Field asks for Contacts access so it can first check whether that person is already in your address book and offer to update that entry instead of creating a duplicate. With your permission it then writes the contact to your iPhone Contacts, either as a new entry or by updating the one you chose. That check runs on your device and looks only for the person you are adding. WP Field never uploads your address book. The only address-book information that reaches our servers is what you choose to import into a customer record, or the details you confirm when merging. If you decline Contacts access, WP Field skips the duplicate check and simply creates a new entry through Apple's own contact editor. Contact information is used only for Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s internal sales records. It is never sold, and it is never used for advertising or tracking.

Camera and Business Card Images: Scanning a business card is the only thing WP Field uses your camera for. While the scanner is open, the camera preview looks for text on the card, and when you tap to scan it captures one photo. Both the preview and the photo are read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is never uploaded to our servers, and WP Field does not save it to your photo library or anywhere else on your device. It is held only while that scan is open, so you can check a field against the card while you correct it, and it is released when you close the scan. Only the text you keep is saved, as contact details on the customer account. WP Field has no access to your photo library at all, and it never uses the camera in the background.

Voice Input: When you dictate notes or log a report by voice, whether in the app, through Siri, or in CarPlay, WP Field uses your microphone and Apple's on-device speech recognition to transcribe what you say. The audio stays on your device; only the resulting text is saved, as part of your call report notes.

On-Device Text Features: Some optional features use Apple's on-device and Apple Intelligence text processing (for example, tidying dictated notes or suggesting a call outcome). That processing runs under Apple's own privacy policy, and Wear Parts & Equipment Co. does not receive your notes or call content through it.

Notifications and Push: With your permission, WP Field sends reminders for follow-ups and delivers messages from your sales management team. To send push notifications, your device token is stored on our servers and linked to your account; it is used only to deliver these notifications, never for marketing, and is not shared with anyone. Reminder text is generated on your device from your report data. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Diagnostic and Usage Telemetry: WP Field sends diagnostic and usage events to our servers, such as app errors, sync failures, location-permission status, and feature-engagement events (which features you open and the actions you take in them). This helps us fix problems and improve the app. These events are linked to your account so we can trace a problem to the device it happened on; they never include your report content. These events also include your iPhone's model and iOS version, so we can reproduce a problem on the same kind of device.

Administrative Messages: Broadcast messages your administrator sends to reps are stored on our servers for delivery and kept for their expiry period (typically 24 hours). They contain no personal information about you.

Calendar: If you save a follow-up to your calendar, WP Field asks for calendar access. It reads only the names of your calendars, so you can choose where reminders go, and it writes only the reminder events it creates, updating or removing them when you change or delete the follow-up. If you choose "Add Events Only," WP Field can't change an event after adding it, so you'll need to remove it in Calendar yourself. Your other events are never read, searched, or changed.

Prospect Nudges: If you turn on prospect nudges (they're off by default), WP Field shows prospects you haven't been back to in a while on your Today screen, with an optional weekly reminder. This list is worked out on your iPhone and is never sent to our servers or shared with your managers.

CarPlay: In a supported vehicle, WP Field shows a simplified interface for your assigned customers, recent reports, follow-ups, and nearby prospects, and lets you log quick check-ins by voice. It only displays data already in the app and collects nothing new; your iPhone remains the source of all data, and nothing is stored on the vehicle.

How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely for Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s internal business operations: recording and reviewing sales activity, tracking customer visit locations, and managing follow-ups and reporting.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on secure servers operated by Wear Parts & Equipment Co. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, is never used for advertising or tracking, and WP Field uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Background Activity

WP Field may sync data in the background to keep your customer list and reports current. It does not read your location in the background.

Account Deletion and Your Choices

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This removes your name and email address from our servers. Call report content you have submitted is kept as a business record under Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s retention policy. To request manual deletion or ask any data-related question, contact us below.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your employment relationship with Wear Parts & Equipment Co. continues, or as needed for business record-keeping.
