Privacy Policy

WP Field — Wear Parts & Equipment Co.

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Overview

WP Field is a private enterprise application developed by Wear Parts & Equipment Co. for internal use by its sales team. It is distributed through the Apple App Store as an unlisted app — available only by direct link to authorized Wear Parts & Equipment Co. personnel, and not shown in public App Store search, categories, or charts.

Data We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with your Apple ID, we receive your name and email address, which identify your account and associate your activity within the app.

Location Data: For in-person call reports, WP Field records your location so the report reflects where the visit took place. That location is saved with the report and synced to our servers — as map coordinates and, when it can be resolved, a street address (looked up through Apple's location services). The app also notes basic details about the reading — how accurate and how recent it was, and whether location access was on — and if no location is available, the report still saves and is marked unverified. Location is used the same way when you open Nearby Customers or use CarPlay to surface nearby accounts and prospects. WP Field never reads your location in the background or continuously — only when you create an in-person report or open Nearby Customers.

Call Report Content: Notes, customer selections, call outcomes, and follow-up items you enter are stored as part of your call reports.

Voice Input: When you dictate notes or log a report by voice — in the app, through Siri, or in CarPlay — WP Field uses your microphone and Apple's on-device speech recognition to transcribe what you say. The audio stays on your device; only the resulting text is saved, as part of your call report notes.

On-Device Text Features: Some optional features use Apple's on-device and Apple Intelligence text processing (for example, tidying dictated notes or suggesting a call outcome). That processing runs under Apple's own privacy policy, and Wear Parts & Equipment Co. does not receive your notes or call content through it.

Notifications and Push: With your permission, WP Field sends reminders for follow-ups and delivers messages from your sales management team. To send push notifications, your device token is stored on our servers and linked to your account; it is used only to deliver these notifications, never for marketing, and is not shared with anyone. Reminder text is generated on your device from your report data. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Diagnostic and Usage Telemetry: WP Field sends diagnostic and usage events to our servers — such as app errors, sync failures, location-permission status, and anonymous feature-engagement metrics (which features you open and the actions you take in them). This helps us fix problems and improve the app, and never includes your report content or other personal information.

Administrative Messages: Broadcast messages your administrator sends to reps are stored on our servers for delivery and kept for their expiry period (typically 24 hours). They contain no personal information about you.

Calendar: If you save a follow-up as a calendar event, WP Field asks for calendar access to create that one event; it does not read or change your existing calendar events, and the event is removed when you delete the reminder.

CarPlay: In a supported vehicle, WP Field shows a simplified interface for your assigned customers, recent reports, follow-ups, and nearby prospects, and lets you log quick check-ins by voice. It only displays data already in the app and collects nothing new; your iPhone remains the source of all data, and nothing is stored on the vehicle.

How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely for Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s internal business operations — recording and reviewing sales activity, tracking customer visit locations, and managing follow-ups and reporting.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on secure servers operated by Wear Parts & Equipment Co. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and WP Field uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Background Activity

WP Field may sync data in the background to keep your customer list and reports current. It does not read your location in the background.

Account Deletion and Your Choices

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This removes your name and email address from our servers. Call report content you have submitted is kept as a business record under Wear Parts & Equipment Co.'s retention policy. To request manual deletion or ask any data-related question, contact us below.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your employment relationship with Wear Parts & Equipment Co. continues, or as needed for business record-keeping.
